Aam sawalat

Jo sab se zyada poochha jata hai.

Meri tasveer kahan jati hai?

Model tumhare browser mein hi chalta hai. Lekin jab tasveer pehle se yaad na ho, to wo Gemini (Google) ko bheji jati hai taake wo tafseel bataye. Yaadasht mein sirf jawab ka text aur tasveer ka aik adadi nishan save hota hai, tasveer khud nahi.

Jawab ghalat ho to kya karun?

"Ghalat hai?" wala button dabao, Gemini dobara dekhega. Ya apna sahi jawab likh kar "Ye yaad kar lo" dabao. Agli baar isi tarah ki tasveer par wahi jawab aayega.

Pehli baar der kyun lagti hai?

Model pehli baar download hota hai. Net slow ho to kuch der lagti hai. Is dauraan bhi tasveer chun sakte ho, Gemini ka jawab model ke bina bhi aata hai.

Kaun si cheezein nahi pehchanta?

Model ki 1000 qisam hain. Jo cheez us list mein nahi, us par wo kuch na kuch qareebi jawab de deta hai. Aisi surat mein Gemini ka jawab zyada bharosemand hota hai. Jab model ko kam yaqeen ho to page khud yaad dilata hai.
